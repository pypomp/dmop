\begin{figure}[p]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{imgs/benchmarks/final_likelihoods.pdf}
\caption{Final log-likelihoods from repeated parameter searches.
Each point is one search: 20 per method in panels A--D and 100 in panel E.
Boxes show the median and interquartile range; whiskers extend to the most
extreme values within 1.5 interquartile ranges of the box.
All estimates are shown, including those outside the whiskers.
Crosses mark searches that stopped prematurely, evaluated at their last
eligible estimate. Dashed lines mark the analytic maximum in panels A and B
and the best displayed estimate in panels C--E.
Likelihoods are evaluated independently of fitting, using the same model
within each panel. All axes are linear.}
\label{fig:benchmark-final-likelihoods}
\end{figure}

\begin{figure}[p]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{imgs/benchmarks/optimization_detail.pdf}
\caption{Optimization progress near the final estimates.
Solid lines show median log-likelihoods and shaded regions span the 10th
through 100th percentiles across searches, with the same shading opacity
as Figure~\ref{fig:benchmark-optimization-full}.
Intermediate likelihoods are evaluated at saved checkpoints; these
evaluations do not select the fitted parameter estimates.
Values are interpolated linearly between checkpoints, and each search's
final value is retained after it stops.
Each panel uses a linear vertical scale restricted to the region near the
final estimates. Figure~\ref{fig:benchmark-optimization-full} shows the full
range of these trajectories and percentile bands.
The starting points, reference lines and final evaluations are those of
Figure~\ref{fig:benchmark-final-likelihoods}.}
\label{fig:benchmark-optimization-detail}
\end{figure}
